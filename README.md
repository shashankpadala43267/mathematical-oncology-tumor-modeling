# Computational Modeling of Tumor Growth, Treatment Dynamics, and Drug Resistance

## Project Overview

This independent computational research project explores mathematical oncology through ordinary differential equations, numerical methods, and Python-based simulations.

## Research Objectives

- Compare exponential, logistic, and Gompertz tumor growth models.
- Investigate numerical accuracy using Euler's method.
- Analyze the effects of different drug kill rates.
- Compare continuous and intermittent treatment schedules.
- Conduct parameter sensitivity analyses.
- Model competition between drug-sensitive and drug-resistant tumor populations.

## Limitations

This project uses hypothetical parameters rather than clinical or experimental tumor data. The simulations are intended for educational and exploratory research purposes and do not provide clinically validated predictions or treatment recommendations.
